STAGE 1

The menu is a street.

Every system is a shop and your games are on its shelves. A banana walks you there.

Left and right walk one shop. L and R jump to the next maker's block. A shop with no games has its shutter down. Press A on a game and it starts in the Super NES's mosaic: the picture breaks into bigger and bigger blocks while the light steps down in sixteenths.

The street follows the clock, too. Dawn from 5, day from 8, dusk from 17, night from 20. The windows come on at dusk.

STAGE 2

Pick your ride.

Three styles, one handheld. Banana Blast is the Genesis one: a neon course at night, a billboard for every system and the banana on a skateboard. Its blacks are true black, so the AMOLED's pixels stay off. Pick a palette and the whole page follows.

Genesis

Banana Blast. Options is an arcade settings screen with a Sound Test, search is a high-score name entry, and a game starts with a palette fade and a line-scroll wobble. Its own FM sounds and a funk loop.

SNES

Banana Street and its five neighborhoods. 15-bit colour, scanline skies, parallax and one real pixel grid: 310 × 270, 4 × 4 panel pixels per art pixel.

Classic

The menu from before 0.7: a carousel of chunky tiles with a gold highlight, in seven colour themes and six four-shade LCD ones. Themes are JSON. Make your own.

STAGE 3

Under the hood.

Early access, built from source. The panel timings, the amplifiers, the DSP graph and the sleep behaviour were all measured on a real handheld.

⇄

A/B boot slots

An update is written to the other slot, never the one you're running. A bad update just boots the old one.

▣

Its own compositor

banana-ui owns the screen while you play. Games are Wayland clients, so the pause menu and cards sit above any game, port or video.

✎

Drawn on the CPU

The whole menu is painted by hand into KMS dumb buffers. No toolkit, no browser, no Electron.

♫

PipeWire, no systemd

PipeWire and WirePlumber run the sound. The first boot chime went from about 20 seconds to about 8.

◐

Free shaders

CRT, aperture-grille and LCD masks ride the overlay plane, so they cost the GPU nothing, over the menu and every game.

✓

Tested every build

Every image CI makes is booted in QEMU and tested before it ships. Sleep keeps your game exactly where it was.
